A11y Learning: Making the Digital World Welcoming

With A11y Learning, you can take the resources of A11y Learn with you anywhere! Our PWA is designed to work offline, so you can access our educational content even without an internet connection. Whether you're on a train, in a park, or simply away from Wi-Fi, A11y Learning ensures that you can continue your journey towards mastering accessibility in software development. Download our app today and make the digital world more welcoming for everyone!

Don't need the mobile app? You can access the web version here!

How to Download A11y Learning

To download A11y Learning, follow these steps:

  1. Visit the A11y Learn website on your mobile browser of choice
  2. Click on the "Share" button. In Chrome, this is in the top right corner by the search bar. In Safari, it's in the bottom right corner, after clicking the 3 dots.
  3. Find and press the "Add to Home Screen" option. You should get a popup. Ensure that "Open as Web App" has been enabled.
  4. Press the "Add" button, and you're done!
  5. Now you can access A11y Learning directly from your home screen, and it will work offline for easy access to our educational content anytime, anywhere!
